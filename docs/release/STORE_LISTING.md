## App-naam (max 30)

`SAPIENTIA` (9) — of `Sapientia: The Forgotten Archives` (33, te lang; dan `Sapientia: Forgotten Archive` = 28).

## Korte beschrijving (max 80)

`A 16-bit RPG about a library that remembers you. Explore, fight, catch monsters.` (79)

Alternatief: `Pixel-art RPG. Six Archives, one Voice. Find out why it knows your name.` (72)

## Volledige beschrijving (max 4000)

```
SAPIENTIA is a 16-bit style RPG for your phone, played in landscape, one hand on the joystick.

You are Dax. You grew up in the Watcher's House with your grandfather, next to a library nobody enters: the First Archive. One night its seal breaks, pink light flickers between the shelves, and your grandfather runs inside. You follow.

What you find is not just a library. The Old Humanity built six Archives to keep what they knew. Something has been living inside them ever since. It speaks. It knows your name. It calls itself Sapientia.

EXPLORE
- A hand-drawn pixel world: the village of Willowdale, the farms of Mapleton, the Dark Wood, the Hollow Mountain and the overgrown greenhouse of the Living Archive.
- Dungeons with real puzzles: pushable cabinets, pressure plates, levers, growing plants and sealed gates.
- Books that glow, notes left by people who came before you, and a village that slowly tells you what it is afraid of.

FIGHT
- Action battles on the map: dodge, swing, block and chain attacks, or switch to classic turn-based combat in the settings.
- Bosses with phases, telegraphed attacks and a weak point you have to earn.
- Five heroes with their own weapons and spells, four of them in the party at once, the fifth in reserve.

CATCH
- Weaken a monster and write it into a Blank Book. It joins your party as a Codex ally with its own skill: poison, bind, pull, counter.
- Fill the Codex. Some creatures only appear once.

GO DEEP
- The Deep Learning Simulation: a hundred floors that are different every run. Blue chests keep their loot between runs. Everything else stays below.
- A merchant who has forgotten his own name, and who seems to know yours.

MADE FOR PHONES
- 384x216 pixel-perfect rendering, scaled sharp to any screen.
- Touch controls built for thumbs: joystick, run button, three action buttons.
- Save anywhere a book glows. Autosave when you switch apps.
- No ads, no accounts, no internet needed. Your save stays on your device.

Sapientia is made by EazyD Studio, a one-person indie studio. This is World 1 of a larger story; the next Archives are in the works.
```

(ongeveer 1.900 tekens; ruim onder 4000)

## Wat de teksten vermijden (canon)

- Geen "H-6", geen "test subject", geen "the Pendant is the Key": dat is de onthulling van Archief 4-5.
- Geen "Sapientia is an AI" als feit: de store-tekst zegt "something", "it speaks".
- Geen namen van WM2/WM3-plekken.
